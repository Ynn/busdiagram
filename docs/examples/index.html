    Examples

    #Examples

    Each example is a diagram of a typical installation or an integration pattern. Every example page shows the diagram, which can be operated, with HTML and JSON tabs to inspect or copy the code and a link to open it in the designer. The scenario files are also published as JSON in the scenarios/ directory of the site.

    #Installations

    Control several outputsOne push-button and one four-output actuator; a single telegram switches several outputs. Status feedbackTwo toggle keys on the same output, with and without status feedback. Shutter controlLong press moves the shutter, short press stops it or steps the slats; an IP supervisor shows the position. Venetian blindLong press moves the blind after the slats have turned; short press turns the slats; slat angle setpoint and feedback. DimmingA dimming actuator driven by switching, relative dimming, and an absolute level. Tunable whiteA dimming actuator adjusts the level and the colour temperature of a tunable white light. Push-button interfaceA push-button interface measures presses itself: one-key dimming and blind, and a scene stored by a long press. ScenesOne scene address recalls a preset on each actuator channel. TimersTimed outputs, a clock, and a presence detector handled by the actuator. Priority controlA priority command overrides normal switching until it is released.

    #Systems and mechanisms

    Miscalibrated shutterThe actuator estimates the shutter position from its configured travel time; compare it with the actual position. Flags W and TIgnored writes and blocked transmissions on a six-output actuator. Full topologyTwo areas coupled by KNXnet/IP routers, with line couplers, a line extension, and a supervisor. DALI gatewayA KNX/DALI gateway translates telegrams into commands for two DALI groups and reports ballast faults. Heating per roomPI and two-point room control, PWM valve actuation, window contact, and operating modes. Heating and coolingAutomatic and object change-over of a room thermostat, a change-over valve, and fan coils. Weather protectionA weather station raises the shutter on wind; a logic module applies sun protection in automatic mode. Air qualityA CO₂ sensor controls a ventilation fan in three steps and sets CO₂ and humidity alarms. Energy meteringA switching actuator with metering reports power and energy per output, and sheds a load above a power limit. Heat pump behind a gatewayA heat pump and a hot-water tank controlled by their own system: a gateway links them to KNX, and an independent meter measures them. Time scheduleA simulated clock drives a clock master, a weekly time switch, and a night time window in a logic module. USB interface toolGroup writes and reads from a commissioning computer connected through a USB interface. Bus voltage failureCut and restore the bus voltage of a line; lock an output and delay its switching. Intrusion and fire alarmsStore intrusion and fire alarms until they are reset; make a light blink or force it on. Heat pump and hot waterSwitch a heat pump from a room thermostat; heat a hot water cylinder with its own thermostat.
