    #Miscalibrated shutter

    The actuator is configured for a 20-second travel time, but the real shutter needs 30 seconds. For a 50% target, the actuator drives the motor for 10 seconds and reports an estimated position of 50%; the real shutter has moved only about 33%. Select the actuator to compare both positions. Then try the correct 30-second setting.
